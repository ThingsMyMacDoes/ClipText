This Privacy Policy explains how ClipText for macOS ("ClipText", "the app") handles information. ClipText is published by Michael Crombeen ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • ClipText does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It cannot connect to the internet: its macOS sandbox does not allow network access.
  • Text recognition happens on your Mac. The screen area you select is processed in your Mac's memory using Apple's built-in Vision framework. The resulting text is placed on your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • ClipText stores none of your captures. It keeps no history and no files. It only remembers two settings on your Mac (see section 3).
  • We only receive personal data if you contact us (for example by email), or, in limited form, through Apple or our website host, as described in section 4.

3. What happens inside the app

When you start a capture by clicking the menu bar icon, choosing "Capture Text", or pressing the keyboard shortcut ⇧⌘2:

  1. ClipText makes a temporary copy, in your Mac's memory, of what is currently on your clipboard, so it can put it back if no text is found.
  2. macOS's built-in screenshot tool lets you select an area of the screen. You can cancel with Esc; your clipboard is then left untouched.
  3. macOS places an image of that area on your clipboard. ClipText reads it into memory.
  4. ClipText recognises the text in the image on your Mac, using Apple's Vision framework.
  5. If text is found, ClipText replaces the clipboard content with the recognised text and briefly shows a preview (up to two lines) near the top of your screen for about three seconds. You can turn this preview off with Show Text Preview in the right-click menu.
  6. If no text is found or an error occurs, ClipText shows a message and puts back the clipboard content you had before the capture.

Confirmation messages are also announced to VoiceOver, if you use it. With the preview turned off, the recognised text is not shown or announced.

Anything visible on your screen may end up in the selected area, including personal or sensitive information. That is why this processing:

  • happens only when you start it,
  • happens only in your Mac's memory,
  • is never transmitted to us or any third party by ClipText,
  • is never stored by ClipText. Once a capture is finished, ClipText keeps no copy of the image, the text or your previous clipboard content.

Your clipboard: your clipboard is managed by macOS, not ClipText. Other apps on your Mac can read it. If you use Apple's Universal Clipboard (Handoff), macOS may make clipboard content available on your other Apple devices. Clipboard-manager apps you install may keep a history. These features are controlled by you and by macOS.

Keyboard shortcut: ClipText registers the shortcut ⇧⌘2 with macOS. macOS tells ClipText only when that exact shortcut is pressed. ClipText does not see or record any other keystrokes, and needs no Accessibility permission.

Settings stored on your Mac: ClipText saves two settings in its app container on your Mac, using standard macOS preferences: whether the text preview is shown, and whether the welcome window has already been shown. They contain no personal data and are never sent anywhere.

Links: the Privacy Policy and Support items in the right-click menu open our website in your web browser. ClipText itself does not connect to the internet. Visiting the website is covered in section 4.

Permissions: macOS asks for Screen Recording permission the first time you capture, because reading screen contents requires it. macOS may also ask before ClipText reads the clipboard (ClipText reads it to save your previous content and to read the screenshot). You can withdraw these permissions at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Without Screen Recording permission, captures will not contain readable text.

Because no data from the app reaches us, we have no app data that we could disclose, sell, share or use for advertising or profiling.

9. Security

  • ClipText runs in Apple's App Sandbox, with network access disabled. It does not open, read or write your files.
  • Captured images, recognised text and the temporary copy of your previous clipboard content are kept only in memory during a capture and are never written to disk by ClipText. (Like any app's memory, macOS may temporarily move it to its encrypted swap file.)
  • We keep support correspondence in an account protected by a strong password and two-factor authentication. [ACTION REQUIRED: Confirm]

Please be careful when capturing sensitive information such as passwords. The text will be on your clipboard and may briefly appear in ClipText's on-screen preview, which others can see, for example during screen sharing. Turn off Show Text Preview to avoid this.

10. Children

ClipText is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone through the app. If a child contacts us by email, we will use their message only to respond. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete it.

We will publish the updated version at https://thingsmymacdoes.github.io/ClipText/privacy with a new "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will also mention the change in the App Store release notes. Previous versions are available on request.
