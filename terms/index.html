2. What ClipText does

ClipText is a menu bar utility. It lets you select an area of your screen, recognises the text in that area on your Mac, and copies the text to your clipboard. Its features are described on the App Store product page and in our support pages at https://thingsmymacdoes.github.io/ClipText/support.

Text recognition is automatic and can contain errors. Accuracy depends on text size, sharpness, contrast and language. ClipText asks Vision, the Apple technology it uses, to detect the language automatically; accuracy can still vary between languages. Please check recognised text before relying on it, especially numbers, codes, passwords and anything important.
