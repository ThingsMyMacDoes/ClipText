ClipText

Copy text from anywhere on your Mac's screen.

ClipText lives in your menu bar. Select any area of your screen, including screenshots, images, paused videos, scanned PDFs, or apps that won't let you select text, and ClipText puts the text on your clipboard, ready to paste.

  • Text recognition runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's Vision technology.
  • No internet access, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Requires macOS 27 or later.
